The filename must match the project's `slug` in `src/lib/projects-data.ts` — that is what
links the card to this page, and what makes the homepage "Case study" link appear.

Keep drafts as `draft: true` until every number and claim in them is one you can defend in
an interview. A thin case study is worse than no case study.

## The problem

What was actually hard. Not what the product does — the constraint that made it
non-obvious. Name the pressure: a deadline, a legacy system, a cost, a rule you could not
break.

## How it works

The architecture decision worth explaining, and what it cost. Two or three layers is
plenty. Say why this shape and not the obvious alternative.

## What the design buys

Three bullets, each a consequence rather than a feature. "Rebuilds are a test" is a
consequence; "uses Kubernetes" is a feature.

## What I would change

The honest retrospective. This is the section readers trust most, because it is the one
that cannot be copied from a landing page.
